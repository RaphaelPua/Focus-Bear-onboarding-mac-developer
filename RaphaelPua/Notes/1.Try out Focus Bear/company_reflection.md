# Why do you think Focus Bear was created?
- Focus Bear was created as a tool to help people with autism and ADHD to help them focus and complete their tasks, routine, and work. It was made to not only help people develop good habits but also help people to complete to effectively complete their tasks throughout the day. I believe that this goal is a very good one as with the current rise of technology, there is also a rise in bad habits. For instance, I myself have started to stare at my screen for too long which have been affecting my vision, Focus Bear has these features which help with reminding me on when I need to take a break for my eyes.

# What problem is Focus Bear Solving?
- The main problem that Focus Bear is solving is productivity issues. Which can either be due to the lack of control or being unable to properly follow a routine which is essentially impacting their physical and mental health. It help solves these issues by blocking apps, enforcing routines to the user, and keeps the person on track by showing listed tasks. I can relate to this because I sometimes struggle with procrastination and staying focused on my tasks. For example, I sometimes put off tasks that I know I need to complete and end up having to rush them closer to the deadline. This can make it harder to manage my time consistently.

# Why do you think this mission is important?
- Focus Bear's mission is an important one as it tries to help people with Autism, ADHD, or are just neurodivergent, or just people having difficulty in focusing and being productive. Thus, Focus Bear's work and mission helps in enhancing these people's ability to focus, create a routine, be healthy, and be productive with their lives. Helping people just also provides an overall positive effect to society as well. I can relate to this as there are many general habits that I need to improve on, such as my sleeping habits, my eating, and fixed my habit of procrastination. For instance, I also sometimes find myself getting immersed on my work or on what I am doing which impacts my eating and sleeping habits.
