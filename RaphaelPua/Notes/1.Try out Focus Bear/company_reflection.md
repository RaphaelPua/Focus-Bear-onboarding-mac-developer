# Why do you think Focus Bear was created?
- Focus Bear was created as a tool to help people with autism and ADHD to help them focus and complete their tasks, routine, and work. It was made to not only help people develop good habits but also help people to complete to effectively complete their tasks throughout the day.

# What problem is Focus Bear solving?
- The main problem that Focus Bear is solving is productivity issues. Which can either be due to the lack of control or being unable to properly follow a routine which is essentially impacting their physical and mental health. It help solves these issues by blocking apps, enforcing routines to the user, and keeps the person on track by showing listed tasks.

# Why do you think this mission is important?
- Focus Bear's mission is an important one as it tries to help people with Autism, ADHD, or are just neurodivergent, or just people having difficulty in focusing and being productive. Thus, Focus Bear's work and mission helps in enhancing these people's ability to focus, create a routine, be healthy, and be productive with their lives. Helping people just also provide an overall positive effect to society as well.
