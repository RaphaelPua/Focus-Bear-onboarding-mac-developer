## Cold Turkey
- Only for desktop, and it blocks apps, websites and the internet completely until the timer runs out
- Provides cross-device syncing 
- It is more focused on purely blocking and forcing you to do something else

## Freedom
- Has cross device syncing too
- Has blocking features AND helps with scheduling, and provides ambient music

## Tiimo
- More of a visual planner and timer tool
- Not much blocking features
- really good for planning and keeping things on track

## Routinery
- Provides step-by-step routines that are timed, giving a highly structured daily routine
- Help with habits, routines, and provide you with routine statistics
- Not much distraction blocking though


# What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?

It has the ability to block apps and websites, provides you timed routines, allows you to reflect on your habits, provide focus music, can be synced with other devices, and list down tasks you need to acomplish.
