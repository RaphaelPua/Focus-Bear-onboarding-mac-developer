# Focus Bear Feature Frustrations

- A current frustration I have is with the habits of the app, it forces you to take them before being able to use my laptop. Which at first make sense as it does help and forces you to build certain habits that you want to create. But as a first time user, it may be too rigid as starting habits would lock you out of your computer until the timer ends. Most of the time I tick the habits completed instead of actually starting the habit and letting the timer play it out. Thus, this feature becomes a bit useless to me, but it may still be important for people who really need that rigid enforcement.
