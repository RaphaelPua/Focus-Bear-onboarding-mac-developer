# App notes
- Focus bear is more than just an blocker for you to focus, it also helps with building habits for the user so that they are able to focus and build a healthier lifestyle
- Cross Device function, where the app works on sync across platforms such as desktop, IOS, and Android. Just logging in the same account would sync your devices.
- Distraction blocking: can be used to block both apps and websites. Thus the app itself would need blocking permissions, user authentications, security and privacy concerns.
- Morning and evening routines: The app allows users to add routines that Focus Bear asks the person to accomplish. These routines can be further customized to include instructions, images, and even youtube videos.

# What I learned that I did not know
- Looking through the help page, I did not realised that Focus Bear has a feature to use AI to help you identify apps and websites that it can recommend to block so you would not get distracted. I also did not realised that it can detect meetings which essentially stop the microbreaks from happening and interrupting an important meeting, and that microbreaks would also have dedicated youtube tutorials for you to kind of use to practice what you are doing during the break or just provide you with more information.
