## Benefits and Challenges of Agile

- Benefits of Agile includes flexibility, transparency, collaboration, and continous improvement. Agile aids in allowing the team to quickly respond to changing requirements.
- However with agile, priorities can frequently change which can make it difficult for the team to maintain focus on work. Agile would also need to have strong communication and discipline among its team and members. If members do not update their tasks or communicate our issues or blockers, the workflow can be stopped or become unclear.

## Difference between Kanban and Scrum

- Scrum is agile but still follows a structure that is made up of fixed sprints. These fixed sprints are essentially include the work needed to be done and is planned during sprint planning. With Kanban, it focuses more on the workflow and moving tasks from one area of workflow to another, it helps us understand where the current task is and know which task to prioritize right now.
