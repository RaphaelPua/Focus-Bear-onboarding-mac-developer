## Communication and Alignment

- Agile ceremonies would provide me with regular opportunities to communicate my progress, clarify priorities, identify blockers, and discuss potential improvements and solutions. They are a structured way for me to stay aligned on what needs to be done and improve my understanding of things.

## Most important for my role

- I believe that daily stand-ups are the most important ceremony for my role because they allow me to quickly understand what the team is working on while also giving me an opportunity to communicate my own progress and blockers.
- They also make it easier to ask for help when I encounter an issue rather than spending too much time trying to solve the problem on my own. This is especially useful as an intern because I may not always know the best approach to solving a problem, and communicating early can prevent me from spending unnecessary time going in the wrong direction.

## Improve my team collaboration

- One change I can make to improve team collaboration is to communicate with my teammates more regularly outside of the daily stand-up meetings. At the moment, we mostly work independently and meet during the daily stand-up to provide updates.
- I think having more conversations outside of these meetings would help us become more comfortable asking each other for help and discussing problems as they come up. It would also allow us to share ideas and knowledge more frequently instead of waiting until the next stand-up.
- This could make our work more efficient and help us maintain a more consistent quality of work across the team.
