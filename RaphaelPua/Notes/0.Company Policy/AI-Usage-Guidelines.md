## When to use AI?

- I should only use AI at the very beginning stages of the task or the project. SPecifically heping me understand new concepts, brainstorming, looking at different approaches, and making drafts. It is also good with helping with repetitive tasks. However, I should rely on my own skills when technical decisions, solving complex problems, communicating with my teammates, and handling sensitive information. Thus, I should ensure I understand the code and content created by AI before I use it.

## How to not over-rely on AI while still benefiting from it?

- Definitely trying to solve the problem on my own first would be best before using any AI for help.
- Using AI for learning instead of creating the answers
- Always testing and reviewing the output of AI
- Reflecting on why the solution works instead of accepting it as it is 

## What steps will you take to ensure data privacy when using AI tools?

- I will put confidential information in public AI tools
- I will remove sensative information before entering into the AI
- Follow Focus Bear's privacy and confidentiality policies
- Check if the AI I am using is safe and approved by the team
- Be careful on what information I give the AI

## Better Learning

With AI, I would definitely use it to help me learn Swift better and faster. I can use it to improve my understanding of concepts.

## Best Practice

Focus on solving the problem myself, and enusring that I understand the code and how it works. I should be the one to get to the final answer and not let AI be the final answer. I would need to always keep in mind to avoid sharing personal, sensitive, and confidential information to the AI.
