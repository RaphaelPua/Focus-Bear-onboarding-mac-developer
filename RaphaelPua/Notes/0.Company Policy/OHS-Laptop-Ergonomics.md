
# Equipment

The easiest change I can do as of now is to improve my current chair. I recently added a pillow to my chair to provide lumbar support for my back. Helping reduce pressure on my spine and reduce the amount of time I start to slouch. Thus, helping me keep a good posture throughout my work.

# Habit Change

I started to stretch and stand up more every 20-30mins as it helps me alleviate some pain on sitting down too much. I also started to incorporate the 20-20-20 method where every 20mins I would look at an object 20 meters away for 20sec to help reduce eye strain.

Using the Focus Bear app is a great tool as not only does it help me focus on my work and blocking distracting apps, it also helps me track how long I have been working and when i should take short breaks.
