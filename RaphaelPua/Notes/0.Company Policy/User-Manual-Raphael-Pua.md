## How I Work Best
- I do my best work when my environment is good (not too cold or hot), my work station is comfortable for me to work on for hours, and could get snacks and drinks when needed. 
- I struggle when there are people or things distracting me, such as a friend coming over, or constant loud noises when I am working.

## Communication Style
- I prefer messages to be short and direct at times, but for more complex information, I do like getting verbal confirmation so that no misunderstandings happen. 
- The best way to reach me is through: 
   1. Messages
   2. Discord
   3. Email
   4. Scheduled calls
  
- If you need something urgent, please call me as it would be the best to reach me the fastest.

##Collaboration & Teamwork
- I work best in a team when we are able to get to know each other by working together in person more and hanging out together outside of work.
- My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is being unable to get a reply for a teammate on time as it could halt me from continuing with my work.
- If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means I may be digesting what is being discussed, or that I may be unsure if it is the right time for me to speak.

## Focus & Productivity
- I stay focused by keeping my environment at a comfortable level, and potentially playing some music with my earphones to drown-out distractions.
- Things that distract me include good friends, conversations, loud sounds, and entertainment.
- The best way to support my productivity is to keep me accountable, and try avoiding to strike a conversation when I am focused.

## Feedback & Growth
- I like feedback to be private, have some details, and also provide examples so that I can better contextualise and understand the feedback. 
- When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is by telling me or nudging me in the right direction.
- I appreciate it when teammates are on top of their work and would go out of their way to help me. Be it just trying to help me learn or support me in anyway that they can.
