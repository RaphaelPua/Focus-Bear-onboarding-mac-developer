
## How can you adjust your communication style to be more inclusive of neurodivergent users and teammates?
TO be more inclusive I can:
- Use direct and clear language
- Avoiding jargon that are unnecessary
- Complexity is broken down into smaller and more manageable chunks/steps
- Clearly outlining the expectations and deadlines
- Being patient when talking to neurodivergent users and teammates
- Being open to ask and adjust based on the communication style they have

## First-Person Account
- Title: What productivity app features actually help those with ADHD / Autism stay consistent?
- Link: (https://www.reddit.com/r/ProductivityApps/comments/1tnppz6/what_productivity_app_features_actually_help/?utm_source=chatgpt.com&logging_in=true)

- So I read a first-person discussion from Reddit with a user who identifies as having ADHD and Autism, they were trying to find apps and app features that actually helped people with ADHD/Autism as they seem to have trouble staying consistent. The user mentioned how much of the apps they have used would be too complicated, too customisable, or require too much effort to keep using consistently.
- This relates to Focus Bear because adding too many features may actually make the application harder to use for the people it is trying to support. It is important to make features simple and useful rather than adding complexity for the sake of having more functionality.

# My response to the user:
- Yeah I do understand I sometime feel the same way where some productivity tools can have too much features which can feel overwhelming, especially when there are too much setup involved. 
- If you're struggling to stay consistent, I would recommend to focus on just one simple feature that makes your next task easier to start. You can always add more later if you find them useful.
- The goal of a productivity app should be to reduce the effort of getting started, not give you another system that you have to manage. So if something feels unnecessarily complicated, just focus on the key features that really work for you or at least the features that you really need to accomplish your tasks.
